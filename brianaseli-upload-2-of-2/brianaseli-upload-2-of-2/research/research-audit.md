# Research audit — Brian Aseli public footprint

**Research date:** 17 October 2026 (Nairobi)
**Scope:** Open web, Kenyan media publishers, institutional and awards records, YouTube, and the materials Brian supplied (4-page bio PDF, "Moments That Defined Brian Aseli" timeline graphic, nine photographs).
**Data:** Every item below is recorded in `data/media-library.json` (the site's source of truth) and exported to `research/source-register.csv`.

This is not a complete record of every mention of Brian online. It is a dated account of what was found, what was verified, and what could not be reached.

---

## 1. Summary

Brian's broadcasting career is well documented by independent press. His **event-hosting** record is not — only two hosting credits could be verified from public sources, plus one award. The timeline graphic's nine events all appear to be real events, but none of the public coverage found names Brian or his role.

That gap shapes the site: the broadcasting track record carries the credibility load at launch, and a simple data flag lets each event credit go live the moment Brian supplies proof (a programme, organiser post, photo with branding, or an invoice reference he's comfortable citing).

## 5. Conflicts and corrections

1. **Award title.** Bio says "Kenya Theatre Awards — Music Director of the Year (2024)". The official roll of honour says *Best Musical Arrangement/Score/Adaptation* for *Jaboya* (3rd edition). The site uses the official wording.
2. **"Most Promising Star" (2016, Standard Media).** Press reports say he joined Standard Group in 2017. A 2016 Standard award may be correct (e.g., a pre-hire recognition) but needs context.
3. **Current shows.** Bio lists *Vibe Shift* (Nation FM) and *Friday Night Rave* (NTV) as current; both are confirmed in 2025 coverage, nothing contradicts them, but nothing from 2026 confirms they are still running. *Morning Fix* is a past role (2023–c. early 2025).
4. **Reach figures.** Bio claims Youth Café reached "more than one million viewers weekly". No source found. Not published.
5. **Pulse URL slugs** contain 2024 timestamps, but both articles are dated July 2023. Dates in the register follow the article dateline.
6. **Media–CSO Conference edition/date** (see above).
